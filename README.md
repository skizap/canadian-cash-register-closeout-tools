# Canadian Cash Register Close-Out Tools

A private collection of editable cash-count workbooks and employee training material for a Canadian cash register.

## Files

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [`spreadsheets/Fermeture_Caisse_Simple_CA_Francais.xlsx`](spreadsheets/Fermeture_Caisse_Simple_CA_Francais.xlsx) | **Recommended for day-to-day use.** One-sheet, simple French-Canadian cash count. Employees enter quantities only; the file calculates the total to deposit to or take from the safe. |
| [`spreadsheets/Fermeture_Caisse_Quotidienne_CA_Francais.xlsx`](spreadsheets/Fermeture_Caisse_Quotidienne_CA_Francais.xlsx) | Detailed French-Canadian version. It also gives denomination-by-denomination directions for what to remove or add. |
| [`spreadsheets/Cash_Register_Daily_Closeout_CAD_English.xlsx`](spreadsheets/Cash_Register_Daily_Closeout_CAD_English.xlsx) | Detailed English Canadian version. |
| [`training/Fermeture_de_caisse_simple_formation_employes.pptx`](training/Fermeture_de_caisse_simple_formation_employes.pptx) | French-Canadian employee training presentation for the simple workbook. |

## Important operating notes

- All amounts are in **Canadian dollars (CAD)**.
- Pennies are **not** included.
- The default register float is **C$250.00**. It can be changed directly in the workbook if your location uses another amount.
- The spreadsheet files are **fully editable**: there is no worksheet or cell protection.
- In the simple French version, staff enter the count in yellow cells, then follow the green amount to deposit or the orange amount to take from the safe.

## Quick training sequence

1. Give employees the **simple French workbook**.
2. Present `Fermeture_de_caisse_simple_formation_employes.pptx` before their first close-out.
3. Have each employee complete one supervised practice count.
4. Keep the detailed French or English versions available if your team prefers exact denomination guidance.

# Outils de fermeture de caisse — Canada

Collection privée de fichiers Excel modifiables et de matériel de formation pour la fermeture d’une caisse au Canada.

La version recommandée au quotidien est `Fermeture_Caisse_Simple_CA_Francais.xlsx`. Les employés remplissent les quantités dans les cellules jaunes, puis suivent le montant vert à déposer au coffre-fort ou le montant orange à prendre du coffre-fort.
